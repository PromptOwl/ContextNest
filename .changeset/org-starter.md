New `ctx init --starter org`: a governance-first starter for a whole organization. It lays down the Four Rulebooks (standards, skills, playbooks, methodologies) plus strategy, ships the Accountability Method as the filing and approval method, and writes a `stewards.example.yaml` that gives every domain two reviewers. Starters can now ship root files; an existing file is never overwritten.
